Make Me a Hanzi dictionary data, by Shaunak Kishore and contributors
https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi
https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi/blob/master/dictionary.txt

Syng selects dictionary radical fields from dictionary.txt (derived from
Unihan and CJKlib). This dictionary data is licensed under the GNU Lesser
General Public License version 3 or any later version. The complete LGPL
and accompanying GPL texts follow this notice.

Exact source URLs, SHA-256 hashes, the graphics revision, changes and the import
report ship in resources/character-breakdown/manifest.json. The reproducible
importer is scripts/prepare-character-breakdown.mjs in the Syng source release.
