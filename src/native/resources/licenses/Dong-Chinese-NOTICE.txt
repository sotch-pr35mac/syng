Syng selects formation notes, component names and roles, verification flags,
stroke counts and eligible modern vectors. Notes are whitespace-trimmed, not
rewritten. Missing information is not generated. Historical images are omitted.
The adapted Dong Chinese material is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Exact source URLs, SHA-256 hashes, the graphics revision, changes and the import
report ship in resources/character-breakdown/manifest.json. The reproducible
importer is scripts/prepare-character-breakdown.mjs in the Syng source release.
