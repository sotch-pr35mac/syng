One shared item treatment scales to the two-column phone grid.

Exploration, not implementation. The proposal changes the shared ReaderBookCard presentation, not Library navigation or import behavior. Text prefers charcoal, switches to white on dark covers, and falls back to black if needed to meet 4.5:1 against the actual mixed paint. Long titles wrap and may make a cover taller. Edit controls are visual stand-ins; the live component keeps its separate metadata button. Format labels are optional; the current document model has source type and filename, but no reliable author or cover-art field. Future RSS articles may benefit from a separate compact item layout.
