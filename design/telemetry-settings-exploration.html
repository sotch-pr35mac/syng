Privacy and control

We love privacy, and you should too! That's why Syng's telemetry service is designed from the ground up to be open, transparent, and put you in control of your data. Telemetry data is used only to diagnose issues, improve platform compatibility, and better understand app usage. Telemetry data is accessible only to the project maintainer and is never shared with third parties or used for advertising. You can inspect telemetry payloads, and can opt out of telemetry in part or in full at any time.
